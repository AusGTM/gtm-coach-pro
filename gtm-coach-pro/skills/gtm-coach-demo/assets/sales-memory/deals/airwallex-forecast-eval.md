# Airwallex — Forecast Eval

~$80k annual revenue-forecast / pipeline-analytics deal. Closed-**lost** 2026-03-15 to the in-house
build + analyst-team status quo. Champion-strong, economic-buyer-blocked; the reference loss the
Employment Hero deal now mirrors.

## SPICED
- **Situation:** Enterprise cross-border payments fintech, heavily regulated (multi-jurisdiction
  AML/KYC, data-residency). Forecasting lives inside the CFO's org: a ~6-person finance analyst team
  builds a monthly bottoms-up forecast in spreadsheets, reconciled by hand against CRM data, with a
  **Clearwater** BI layer on top for exec/board reporting. Sales org is ~45 reps; VP Sales Rebecca
  Hall owns the number but not the tooling or the budget.
- **Pain:** Rebecca felt it acutely — quarterly forecast routinely off actuals by ~15–20%, manual
  roll-ups eating days each month, and no rep-level pipeline hygiene view. But the pain lived at her
  level only. CFO David Nguyen saw forecasting as an analyst job, not a software category, so the pain
  never reached the person holding the budget. "I feel the forecast pain, but here forecasting is seen
  as an analyst's job, not software." (Rebecca)
- **Impact:** Never quantified to David's satisfaction and so stayed a "nice to have." We had the
  ingredients — forecast miss %, analyst hours, the board-surprise cost of a blown quarter — but never
  converted them into a dollar figure David accepted against the ~$80k spend. He read it as duplicating
  a team he already pays for. Contrast: on the won deals the EB felt a quantified dollar impact
  directly.
- **Critical Event:** **None.** No deadline, no board mandate, no system deprecation — "maybe next
  budget cycle." With nothing forcing a decision, the status quo was always the safe choice.
- **Decision:** Rebecca championed hard but did not hold budget. David (CFO / economic-buyer) was the
  blocker: "This overlaps what my analysts already deliver" and "No budget allocated this cycle." A
  light compliance/security concern (a full vendor review — SOC 2, pen test, AML data handling, data
  residency — estimated at multiple months) stacked on top as a further reason to defer.

## Risks & gaps
- **No critical event** — nothing forced a decision; "next budget cycle" had no date. Root cause of
  the loss.
- **Economic buyer was a blocker** — David engaged late (first joined at the 2026-02-04 demo) with his
  "analysts already do this" frame already formed; we never got a 1:1 to reframe before it hardened.
- **Impact never quantified** — the forecast-miss and analyst-hours cost was never turned into a dollar
  ROI David would accept against the spend.
- **Competitor: in-house build + analyst team** (the incumbent that won), reinforced by an existing
  **Clearwater** dashboard layer that made "we already have this covered" credible.
- **Compliance overhead** — a multi-month regulated-industry vendor/security review gave a low-urgency
  buyer an easy reason to defer.

First discovery with Owen Clarke, recently promoted to Head of Sales at Go1, a corporate-learning
and upskilling SaaS. Inbound off a webinar signup. Owen inherited a 25-rep team where the founder

  sales still largely owner-led, with the founder involved in most late-stage deals. Forecasting
  today is a single spreadsheet Owen maintains manually — pulled from CRM exports each Friday and
  reconciled over the weekend. No forecast cadence, no defined stages beyond "open / won / lost".
- **Pain:** "Before I double the team I want a forecast process that isn't just me in a spreadsheet
  on Sunday night." (Owen) The manual roll-up doesn't survive contact with more reps; Owen can't
  see slippage until deals miss, and the founder and Owen frequently disagree on what will land.
  "Right now the forecast is basically my gut plus whatever the reps tell me on Friday." (Owen)
- **Impact:** Early signal only — not yet quantified. Owen gestured at "a few deals a quarter that
  surprise us either way" but couldn't put a dollar figure or a rep-time number on it this call.
  ASSUMED: forecast error and slipped deals, but no baseline captured yet — flagged to quantify.
- **Critical Event:** A headcount plan to roughly double the team (toward ~45-50 reps) later in the
  year is the loose trigger — Owen wants process in place "before the new people land, not after".
  No confirmed date or board approval yet; treat as unconfirmed.
- **Decision:** Owen is the champion and, given company size, likely the economic buyer — though he
  noted "the founder would want to see it" before any spend. Process is simple; no procurement,
  no security review expected at this size. EB confirmation is an open gap.

# Go1 Discovery — 2026-06-03

**Attendees:** internal jordan-rep | external Owen Clarke (Head of Sales)

## Summary
First discovery with Owen Clarke, recently promoted to Head of Sales at Go1, a corporate-learning
and upskilling SaaS. Inbound off a webinar signup. Owen inherited a 25-rep team where the founder
still owns most of the pipeline calls and forecasting is a spreadsheet he rebuilds by hand every
week. He wants to professionalise the forecast before the team doubles later in the year. Genuine
pain but very early — no numbers attached yet, and the buying process is undefined.

## SPICED captured this call
- **Situation:** SMB corporate-learning SaaS; ~25 reps split across new-business and renewals;
  sales still largely owner-led, with the founder involved in most late-stage deals. Everything
  lives in HubSpot; on Fridays Owen dumps the deals into a Google Sheet and reconciles it over the
  weekend. "There's a tab where I colour the deals green, amber, red — and I'll be honest, that's
  mostly a feeling, it's not coming from a stage or anything." (Owen) No forecast cadence, no
  defined stages beyond "open / won / lost".
- **Pain:** "Honestly I'm not even sure 'forecast' is the right word for what I do — Sunday night
  I've got a coffee and I'm going down the list like, okay, is this one real, is this one real."
  (Owen) The manual roll-up doesn't survive contact with more reps; Owen can't see slippage until
  deals miss, and he and the founder regularly disagree on what will land. "Right now the forecast
  is basically my gut plus whatever the reps tell me on Friday — and the reps are optimists, every
  single one." (Owen)
- **Impact:** Early signal only — not yet quantified. Owen reached for a couple of moments rather
  than a number: "Last quarter there was a TAFE reseller deal I'd basically written off, and it
  just closed. And then a big one I'd have bet the house on went quiet on me for three weeks. So
  when the founder asks me what's coming, I'm sort of guessing and hoping." Couldn't put a dollar
  figure or a rep-time number on it this call. ASSUMED: forecast error and slipped deals, but no
  baseline captured yet — flagged to quantify.
- **Critical Event:** A headcount plan to roughly double the team (toward ~45-50 reps) later in the
  year is the loose trigger — "I'd rather have something in place before the new people land, not
  be building it while I'm onboarding twenty of them." No confirmed date or board approval yet;
  Owen expects it to firm up after a leadership offsite. Treat as unconfirmed.
- **Decision:** Owen is the champion and, given company size, likely the economic buyer — though
  "the founder would want a look" before any spend. Process is simple; no procurement, no security
  review expected at this size. EB confirmation is an open gap.

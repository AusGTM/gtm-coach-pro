# Objections — rollup

Recurring objections across the call corpus, ranked by frequency, with the response that
worked best (from won deals, with the actual move + evidence) and what to avoid.

## 1. "We already have a tool / BI dashboard" (Clearwater, in-house) — 4 deals
- **Exact buyer language:** *"Clearwater tells me what already happened. I need to know which deals are dying now."* (Sofia, Culture Amp); *"This overlaps what my analysts already deliver."* (David, Airwallex).
- **Best response (won — Culture Amp, 2026-02-10 discovery):** don't argue the tool — reframe the *spend* as a *risk* question. jordan-rep contrasted the cost of one slipped 7-figure deal (the Coles deal, ~$1.2M) against a dashboard licence, and Sofia said it back herself: *"One of those slipping is worth more than every dashboard licence we've ever bought."* Coaching note on that call: this reframe is *"what kept the 'we already pay for Clearwater' objection from hardening."* Complement-then-displace, never rip-and-replace.
- **Avoid:** feature-comparing against the BI tool. That's what lost Airwallex — you must move to deal-level risk, not dashboards.

## 2. "No budget this cycle" / price pushback — 3 deals
- **Exact language:** *"No budget allocated this cycle."* (David, Airwallex); *"Two years is a big commit pre-raise — what's the 1-year option?"* (Nadia, Deputy).
- **Best response (won — SafetyCulture, 2026-04-22 negotiation):** answer commitment risk with a term, not a discount. The stated objection was *"I don't want to lock in for years on something unproven"*; alex-rep met it with a **1-year on-ramp** — full deployment now, single-year commitment, ROI case in hand — which *"removed the risk objection and let the board deadline close the deal"* at $90k ahead of the Q3 board. Deputy carried the same 1-year framing into its pre-raise timeline (Nadia's quote above).
- **Avoid:** discounting before the impact is quantified to the *economic buyer*. Tie price to a recoverable number (slipped pipeline) and a compelling event first.

## 3. "Would this even work with our messy CRM / data?" — 2 deals
- **Exact language:** *"Would this even work with our messy CRM?"* (Tom, Employment Hero).
- **Best response:** lead with a data-readiness checklist; scope cleanup honestly; phase the rollout; don't overpromise day-one accuracy. **Note: not yet resolved in a won deal** — this one lives on the open Employment Hero deal, so treat the response as the recommended play, not a proven win.

## 4. Security / compliance overhead — 2 deals (1 enterprise, 1 regulated)
- **Exact language:** *"How does our employee-PII data stay segregated?"* / *"What data leaves our environment and where does it sit?"* (Ken, Culture Amp; Grace, Employment Hero).
- **Best response (won — Culture Amp, 2026-03-18 technical):** surface security early and let the gatekeeper drive. Priya raised Ken on the demo, not late; on the technical call, Ken's PII/segregation question was answered by *walking the actual read-only Okta scopes* and the tenancy / data-residency story (AU/Sydney), which cleared the gate. Lead with SOC2 + residency + DPA + SSO, then get out of the way.
- **Avoid:** deferring security to a late call — at that point it's a blocker, not a checkbox.

## 5. "Are we too small / too early for this?" — 2 deals (SMB)
- **Exact language:** *"Are we too small for this?"* (Owen, Go1).
- **Best response:** SMB starter framing; keep it simple; anchor to an upcoming trigger (Q4 hiring). **Not yet won** — Go1 is early-stage discovery, so this is the recommended play, not proven.

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals (2 won, 1 lost, 3 open). "Best response (won — …)" lines cite the specific won-deal call; where no won deal covers an objection, it's flagged as a recommended play, not a proven win. Treat single-mention items as weak signal._

# Objections — rollup

Recurring objections across the call corpus, ranked by frequency, with the response that
worked best (from won deals) and what to avoid.

## 1. "We already have a tool / BI dashboard" (Clearwater, in-house) — 4 deals
- **Exact buyer language:** *"Clearwater tells me what already happened. I need to know which deals are dying now."* (Sofia, Culture Amp); *"This overlaps what my analysts already deliver."* (David, Airwallex).
- **Best response (won — Culture Amp):** reframe incumbent as lagging/BI reporting vs deal-level *forward* risk signal. Complement, then displace. Quantify the slipped-deal cost vs the dashboard cost.
- **Avoid:** feature-comparing against the BI tool. It loses (Airwallex) — you must move to deal-level risk, not dashboards.

## 2. "No budget this cycle" / price pushback — 3 deals
- **Exact language:** *"No budget allocated this cycle."* (David, Airwallex); *"Two years is a big commit pre-raise — what's the 1-year option?"* (Nadia, Deputy).
- **Best response (won — SafetyCulture/Deputy):** tie price to a recoverable number (slipped pipeline) and to a compelling event (board meeting, raise). Offer a 1-year on-ramp when multi-year stalls.
- **Avoid:** discounting before the impact is quantified to the *economic buyer*.

## 3. "Would this even work with our messy CRM / data?" — 2 deals
- **Exact language:** *"Would this even work with our messy CRM?"* (Tom, Employment Hero).
- **Best response:** lead with a data-readiness checklist; scope cleanup honestly; phase the rollout. Don't overpromise on day-one accuracy.

## 4. Security / compliance overhead — 2 deals (1 enterprise, 1 regulated)
- **Exact language:** *"What data leaves our environment and where does it sit?"* (Grace, Employment Hero); compliance overhead (Airwallex).
- **Best response (won — Culture Amp):** surface security early, bring the technical gatekeeper in, lead with SOC2 + data residency + DPA + SSO. Let the gatekeeper talk.

## 5. "Are we too small / too early for this?" — 2 deals (SMB)
- **Exact language:** *"Are we too small for this?"* (Owen, Go1).
- **Best response:** SMB starter framing; keep it simple; anchor to an upcoming trigger (Q4 hiring).

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals (2 won, 1 lost, 3 open). Treat single-mention items as weak signal._

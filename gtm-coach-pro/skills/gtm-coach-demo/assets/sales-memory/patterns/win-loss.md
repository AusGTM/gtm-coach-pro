# Win/Loss — rollup

What distinguishes the won deals from the lost one. Small sample (2 won, 1 lost, 3 open) —
treat as directional, not statistical.

## Wins (SafetyCulture, Culture Amp) had in common
- **A real compelling event** — board meeting (SafetyCulture), annual planning (Culture Amp). Both drove signature on a date.
- **The economic buyer engaged early and felt the pain** — Dana (VP Rev), Priya (CRO) were in the room by call 2.
- **Quantified impact in the EB's own terms** — $2.4M slipped (SafetyCulture); two slipped 7-figure deals + board credibility (Culture Amp).
- **Multithreaded** — economic buyer + champion (+ technical gate at Culture Amp).
- **Incumbent reframed, not feature-fought** — Clearwater repositioned as lagging BI (Culture Amp).

## The loss (Airwallex) had
- **No compelling event** — "maybe next budget cycle." Root cause.
- **Economic buyer as blocker** — David (CFO) saw forecasting as an analyst job; never felt the pain.
- **Impact never quantified to the EB** — landed with Rebecca (VP Sales), never with David.
- **Status-quo/in-house build won** — the most dangerous competitor.

## Closed-lost triggers to watch (early-warning checklist)
- No dated compelling event by the demo stage.
- Economic buyer hasn't been on a call / is skeptical.
- Impact quantified only to the champion, not the EB.
- Buyer frames the alternative as "our analysts already do this."

## Open deals showing loss-pattern risk
- **Employment Hero** — EB (CFO Ananya) absent + skeptical, compelling event unconfirmed, in-house path favoured. Same shape as Airwallex. **Highest-priority fix: get the EB in the room with a CFO-grade ROI case.**

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals._

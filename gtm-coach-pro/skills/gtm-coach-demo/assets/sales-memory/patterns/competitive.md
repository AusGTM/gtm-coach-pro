# Competitive — rollup

Competitors named across the corpus, where they show up, why buyers switch, and counters that
won.

## Clearwater (incumbent BI / dashboards) — named in 2 deals (Culture Amp won, mentioned SafetyCulture)
- **Positioning they hold:** established reporting/BI dashboards; "we already pay for it."
- **Why buyers move:** lagging, deal-agnostic. *"Clearwater tells me what already happened."* (Sofia, Culture Amp). Doesn't tell reps which live deals are at risk.
- **Counter that won (Culture Amp):** reframe from competitor to complement → displace. Dashboards report history; Cadence scores forward deal-level risk. Win on "which deals are dying now," not on chart features.
- **Landmine:** getting dragged into a BI feature bake-off. Move the conversation to deal-level risk.

## PipelineIQ (point competitor) — named in 1 deal (Deputy, winning)
- **Why buyers prefer us:** *"weaker on deal-level risk scoring"* (Nadia, Deputy).
- **Counter:** demo the risk-scoring depth side by side; emphasise early-warning signal quality.
- **Sample size: 1 deal — weak signal, watch for more.**

## In-house build / analyst team (status quo) — named in 2 deals (Airwallex lost, Employment Hero open)
- **Why it wins (Airwallex, lost):** CFO viewed forecasting as an analyst job, not software; no compelling event to dislodge the status quo. *"This overlaps what my analysts already deliver."* (David).
- **Counter (must-do):** quantify impact to the *economic buyer* early; establish a compelling event; show analyst time reclaimed + accuracy gain. Where this wasn't done (Airwallex), the status quo won.
- **Open risk (Employment Hero):** Ananya (CFO) currently favours the in-house/analyst path — same pattern that lost Airwallex. Address the EB directly.

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals. The status-quo/in-house "competitor" is the most dangerous — it killed Airwallex and threatens Employment Hero._

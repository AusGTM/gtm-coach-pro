# ICP — rollup

Who actually buys and wins, from the corpus. Directional (small sample).

## Best-fit signals (correlate with wins)
- **Segment:** mid-market to enterprise, 100+ reps, where a single slipped deal is material ($90k–$145k won).
- **Trigger present:** a dated compelling event — board meeting, annual planning, financing round.
- **Economic buyer owns a number:** CRO / VP Revenue who is personally accountable for forecast accuracy (Dana, Priya).
- **Champion in RevOps/Sales Ops** who feels the manual-spreadsheet pain daily (Marcus, Sofia).
- **Existing BI tool but no deal-level signal** — primed for the "forward risk vs lagging reports" reframe.

## Weaker-fit / churn-risk signals
- **Forecasting seen as an analyst's job, not software** (Airwallex) — strong in-house-build culture, regulated, CFO-as-blocker.
- **No compelling event** and impact felt only below the EB.
- **Very small / very early** (Go1, SMB) — viable with a simple starter motion, but quantify impact and confirm a trigger first.

## Fastest / cleanest win profile
Mid-market, 100+ reps, CRO with a board-visible forecast problem, RevOps champion, a dated
compelling event, and a lagging BI incumbent to reframe. (SafetyCulture and Culture Amp both fit.)

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals._

# Messaging — rollup

Value props and framings that landed vs fell flat, with the buyer language to mirror.

## Lands well
- **"Know which deals are dying now" (forward risk, not lagging reports)** — strongest hook across deals. Mirrors *"I need to know which deals are dying now."* (Sofia, Culture Amp). Won the incumbent reframe.
- **"Early warning on slipped pipeline = your forecast gap"** — landed with execs who own a number. *"If we'd caught even half of that $2.4M slipping earlier, that's my whole gap."* (Dana, SafetyCulture).
- **"Board-ready forecast you'd bet on"** — resonates pre-board / pre-raise. *"I can't put a forecast in front of the board I'd bet on."* (Sam, Deputy); *"I lost the room with the board when those two slipped."* (Priya, Culture Amp).
- **Buyer's own error number, reflected back** — *"I'd put the error at 20-30% a quarter."* (Tom, Employment Hero) → 25%→10% framing.

## Falls flat
- **Feature/dashboard comparisons vs BI tools** — loses every time; pulls into the incumbent's frame (Airwallex, Clearwater talk track).
- **Generic ROI without the EB's number** — never landed with David (Airwallex, CFO). Impact must be in *the economic buyer's* terms.

## Persona language cues
- **CRO / VP Rev (economic buyer):** forecast accuracy, board credibility, the gap to number.
- **RevOps / Sales Ops (champion):** deal-level risk signal, less manual spreadsheet work, hygiene.
- **Data Eng / Security (technical gate):** SOC2, data residency, SSO, data segregation.

_Based on 23 calls / 6 deals._

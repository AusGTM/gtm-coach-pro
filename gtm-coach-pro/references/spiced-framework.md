# SPICED Framework & Coaching Rubrics

GTM Coach's default methodology is **SPICED** (Winning by Design) layered with
framework-neutral selling best-practice. Use SPICED to structure deal qualification and
discovery; use the best-practice rubric to evaluate execution on any individual call.

## SPICED

| Letter | Element | What "good" looks like | Diagnostic questions |
|--------|---------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **S** | **Situation** | Clear picture of current state: tools, team, process, status quo, metrics today. | What are they doing today? What's the current stack/process? How big is the team/volume? |
| **P** | **Pain** | A real, owned problem — not a feature wish. Tied to a person who feels it. | What's broken? Who feels it? How often? Why hasn't it been fixed? |
| **I** | **Impact** | The pain quantified: $, time, risk, growth blocked. The cost of inaction AND the value of solving. | What does this cost you? What becomes possible if solved? How are you measuring it? |
| **C** | **Critical Event** | A dated, compelling reason to act by a deadline (renewal, contract end, board mandate, launch, compliance date). | What happens on [date]? What forces a decision? What if nothing changes by then? |
| **D** | **Decision** | Mapped decision process: economic buyer, criteria, competing options, paper/legal/security/procurement steps and timing. | Who signs? What's the process? What criteria? Who else is involved? What could stall it? |

A deal is **well-qualified** only when all five are known, evidenced (ideally with a quote),
and current. Flag any element that is missing, stale, or asserted-without-evidence.

### SPICED health scoring (per deal)
Score each element 0–2: 0 = unknown, 1 = partial/assumed, 2 = confirmed with evidence.
- 9–10 → **green**; 6–8 → **yellow**; ≤5 → **red**.
Always pair the score with the single highest-leverage gap to close next.

## Generic best-practice rubric (per call)

Score 1–5 each; cite evidence (a quote, a timestamp, a moment) for every score.

1. **Discovery quality** — open questions, layered "why", quantified pain/impact, listened
   more than pitched. (Anti-pattern: feature-dumping, happy-ears, no quantification.)
2. **Talk ratio** — rep talk time vs customer. Discovery target: rep ≤ ~45%. Demo can be
   higher but should still pull reactions. Flag monologues > ~2 min.
3. **Multithreading** — more than one stakeholder engaged; explicit effort to reach the
   economic buyer and other influencers. (Anti-pattern: single-threaded on one champion.)
4. **Next-step discipline** — a specific, dated, mutual next step set before the call ends,
   ideally calendar-booked. (Anti-pattern: "I'll follow up", no date.)
5. **Methodology adherence** — advanced at least one SPICED element with evidence; tied
   value to quantified impact; surfaced/confirmed the critical event.
6. **Objection & competition handling** — surfaced concerns rather than dodging; differentiated
   without bashing; traps set for competitors where relevant.

### Talk-ratio estimation
If transcripts are available, estimate talk ratio from speaker-attributed word counts
(rep words / total words). If only summaries exist, mark talk ratio as "n/a — summary only"
and infer engagement qualitatively. Never invent a precise number from a summary.

## Deal-risk signals (for pipeline review & deal-slip warnings)

Raise a risk when any are true:
- No confirmed **Critical Event** (top predictor of slip).
- **Single-threaded** — only one contact across all calls.
- Economic buyer never on a call.
- Impact not quantified (no $ / metric).
- Next step missing, vague, or past due.
- Days-since-last-touch exceeds the deal's stage norm.
- Stage in CRM (if known) ahead of SPICED evidence (e.g. "negotiation" with unknown decision process).
- Competitor mentioned without a confirmed differentiation/counter.
- Sentiment trend declining across calls; champion went dark.

## Coaching output principles

- **Evidence first.** Every observation cites a specific call/quote/metric from the memory
  bank. No generic advice.
- **One thing to change.** End with the single highest-leverage action, not a laundry list.
- **Specific and dated.** Recommendations name who, what, by when.
- **Trend over snapshot.** When scoring a rep, compare to their own history in
  `scorecards/<rep>.md` and report the trajectory.
- **Respect uncertainty.** Distinguish what's confirmed from what's assumed. Don't upgrade a
  guess into a fact.

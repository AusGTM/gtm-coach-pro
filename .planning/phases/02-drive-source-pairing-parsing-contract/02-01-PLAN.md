one thin end-to-end slice: detect a Gemini "Notes by Gemini" doc → export it → role-map its Summary

call schema (memory-bank.md), before Plans 02/03 harden each section.
Output: a new, production-quality (if intentionally thin) `drive-source.md` establishing the spine
and a full worked example.

happy path), TRUST-01 (provenance tag, core).
